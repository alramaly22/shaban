Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This page explains what information this website collects, why it is collected, and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you apply for a coaching plan at checkout, we collect the details the form asks for: your name, email, phone number, age, gender, main goal, training experience, preferred training location, and any notes you add. We also collect your selected plan and chosen payment method.

When you send a message through the Contact page, we collect your name, email, optional phone number, and your message.

We do not collect or store payment card numbers, expiry dates, or CVV codes anywhere on this site.

How we use your information

  • To review and follow up on your coaching application
  • To communicate with you about your plan, your training, and your account
  • To respond to messages sent through the Contact page
  • To keep basic records of orders and communications

We do not sell your information to third parties.

Cookies

This site uses only the minimal cookies needed for it to function - for example, keeping you logged into the admin area and protecting forms from cross-site request forgery. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Data storage

Submitted information is stored in a database and is accessible only to the site administrator. We take reasonable steps to keep it secure, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Third-party services

Payment processing, once a live payment gateway is connected, will be handled directly by that provider under its own privacy policy - this site does not receive or store your card details at any point.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time by reaching out through the Contact page.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.
